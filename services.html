Questions we get asked.

Will agents send emails or messages without asking me?

Not by default. Outgoing messages wait for your approval. Once an agent has proven itself on a low-risk task, you can choose to let it run on its own, and you can switch that off at any time.

How do I know what an agent did?

Each agent keeps a plain-language activity log: what it searched, what it read, what it produced and who approved it. You can read it any time, and you can ask the agent to explain a step.

Which AI models do you use?

We choose models for each task and tell you which ones are involved in your project, so you know where your data goes. We do not tie you to a single provider.

What about my data and privacy?

Agents only access the accounts and files you connect, with the minimum permissions required, and you can revoke access at any time. For personal data we design around the rules that apply in your country, and we recommend you take legal advice for your market.

How long does a pilot take?

It depends on the task and the tools involved. We give you a realistic timeline in the written scope, before any work starts.

What if an agent is not a good fit for my task?

Then we say so on the discovery call. A short honest answer is better for both of us than a project that does not pay off.

Tell us what you want to hand off.

Describe one task that takes too much of your team's time. On a 30 minute call we will tell you honestly whether an agent can do it, and what it would take.
