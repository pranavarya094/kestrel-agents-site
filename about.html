We build AI agents you can supervise.

Northlight Agents exists because most businesses want help from AI, and most are rightly unsure about handing it real work. Our job is to make that safe, visible and worth it.

What we believe

Agents should behave like good colleagues: do the work, show the work, ask before they do anything that matters, and admit when they are unsure.

  • We prepare, you decide.
  • Every action is logged in plain language.
  • Claims come with sources.
  • We start small and grow when it pays off.
  • We say no when an agent is the wrong tool.

What we will not do

Some lines we will not cross, however convenient it would be.

  • Send messages in your name without your approval.
  • Invent sources or present guesses as facts.
  • Hide what an agent did from you.
  • Collect more access or data than a task needs.
  • Promise results nobody can promise.

How we work.

Remote-first and worldwide. We work across time zones, in English, and keep communication short and written down.

Clear scope

Before we build anything, we write down the task, the tools, the definition of success and who approves. You see it first.

Small steps

One agent, tested on real examples, before we add the next. You can stop at any stage.

Honest reporting

You get the logs, not a summary of the logs. If something went wrong, you hear it from us first.

Let's find your first agent.

Describe one task that takes too much of your team's time. On a 30 minute call we will tell you honestly whether an agent can do it, and what it would take.
