Privacy.

Short version: this website does not track you.

This website

We do not use cookies or advertising trackers. Fonts, scripts and images are served from this site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep technical logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Visit notifications

So that we know where our visitors come from, each visit sends us one anonymous notification containing your approximate city and country, the type of device, the page you opened, the time and the website you came from. To work out the approximate city, your browser asks a geolocation service (ipapi.co) using your IP address. We do not receive, store or pass on your IP address (the geolocation service and Discord, which delivers the notification, see the request as any web service does). The notification goes to a private channel that only we can read. Nothing is sent if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. To object, use one of those signals or write to us and we will remove anything linked to you.

If you contact us

The contact form does not send data to a server. It opens your email app with a prepared message, and nothing is sent until you press send. If you write to us, we use what you tell us only to reply to you and to prepare a proposal. We do not sell or share it, and we keep it only as long as needed for that purpose. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to hello@northlightagents.com.

If we work together

Before any project starts, we write down which data agents may access, why, and how access can be withdrawn. Agents use the minimum access needed for the task.

Who is responsible

Northlight Agents is operated by Pranav Arya. Questions about privacy: hello@northlightagents.com.
