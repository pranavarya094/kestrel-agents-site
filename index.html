Six jobs we hand to agents.

Each agent has one clear role, its own tools and its own log. Together they behave like a small team that never forgets a task.

Research

Market scans, competitor briefs and summaries of long documents, with a link behind every claim.

Lead generation

Finds companies that fit your ideal customer, scores them, and drafts personal outreach for you to approve.

Content and social

Articles, newsletters and posts in your voice, edited for search and checked before they go out.

Fact-checking

Reads a draft, checks each claim against sources, and flags what it cannot confirm.

Personal assistants

Inbox triage, scheduling, reminders and follow-ups, prepared for one person or a whole team.

Operations and support

Answers customer questions on WhatsApp, email or your website, and hands the hard ones to a person.

    How a project runs.

    Small start, clear results, then more agents once the first one has proven itself.

    1. Discovery call

      30 minutes, free. We look at your week and pick one repeatable task that costs you hours.

    2. Pilot scope

      We write down what the agent will do, which tools it can use, what a good result looks like, and who approves. Scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts.

    3. Build and test

      We build the agent with your tools and test it on real examples from your business.

    4. Run and review

      You read the logs and approve outputs. We tune the agent until you trust it with that task.

    5. Add teammates

      Once one agent works, we add the next: researcher, writer, checker, outreach.
